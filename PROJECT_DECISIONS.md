# Project decisions and limitations

- Chosen as the third portfolio project to complement sales analytics and inventory risk: finance KPI / budget variance.
- All data is deterministic synthetic data. It is suitable for demonstration and reproducible testing, not business claims.
- Simplified P&L: Revenue - COGS - Operating Expenses = Operating Profit. Taxes, depreciation, interest, accruals, FX translation, eliminations, and multi-currency consolidation are out of scope.
- Variance is Actual - Budget. Revenue and expense variances have different interpretations.
- Birst implementation is described as a build guide; there is no assertion of live tenant deployment.
