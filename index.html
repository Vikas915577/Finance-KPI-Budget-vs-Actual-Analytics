What the sample data suggests

Observations are data-derived; actions are hypotheses to validate.

DISPLAY

Revenue finished above budget

Actual revenue was $38.97M against $38.49M budget; the gap was +$483.6K (+1.26%).

DIAGNOSE

Profit is below plan despite higher revenue

Operating profit was $9.84M against $10.17M budget: −$337.0K (−3.31%). Revenue can beat plan while profit misses if costs rise faster.

Largest favourable department variance: Sales (+$171.4K); largest adverse: Operations (−$284.7K). Investigate drivers; do not assume cause.

DECIDE

Start with Salaries & Benefits

Largest operating-expense variance was +$356.9K. Validate volume, rates, timing and coding with the owner before recommending action.

Metric rule: Revenue variance = actual − budget (positive is generally favourable). Expense variance = actual − budget (positive is generally unfavourable). Operating-profit variance = actual operating profit − budget operating profit.
